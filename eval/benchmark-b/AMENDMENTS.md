## v3 — this version
- **Added four pairs**: business-logic (`ReferralSale*`), economic-attack (`DonationVault*`), cross-contract (`AirdropTrust*`),
  transaction-ordering (`SwapNoSlippage*`). Eight labels appended; the first eight entries of `labels.json` are identical to v2.
- **No v1/v2 contract changed** (verified against `versions/v1` and `versions/v2` manifests).
- **New ground-truth evidence, no label change**: every pair, including the v1/v2 pairs that had been checked only by
  inspection, now compiles under solc 0.8.28 and is verified by execution (`verify-behaviour.mjs`, 8/8).
- **Collision found while authoring (pre-freeze)**: the first cross-contract draft performed the eligibility check before its
  writes; the structural reentrancy detector flagged `AirdropTrustBad.claim` because the call target is caller-supplied. The
  call is to a `view` interface, emitted as STATICCALL, so a callee cannot re-enter and write state: the finding was a false
  positive and a collision with the cross-contract subject. Both variants were reordered to checks-effects-interactions
  (semantics unchanged: the `require` reverts the claim when ineligible), which removes the collision. Recorded as an analyzer
  limitation: the reentrancy heuristic does not distinguish view/STATICCALL targets.
- `verify-behaviour.mjs` and `BENCHMARK.json` added; `verify-behaviour.mjs` is covered by the manifest.
